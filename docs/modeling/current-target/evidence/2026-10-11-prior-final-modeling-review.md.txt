## B. Authority consistency

No unresolved contradiction between active ADR-030–036 policies was found. ADR-036 narrows ADR-033's ordinary exercise retry to exactly one retry after each first error; whole-lesson redo remains a separate new attempt. ADR-036 defers Reassessment, superseding active-purpose interpretations of ADR-032/033/035 while preserving their compatible security/provenance rules. ADR-031's conditional trusted-proficiency evidence concept does not imply Placement or Level-up confers comprehensive proficiency: neither active MVP purpose makes that claim.

ADR-030/031 retain shared curriculum, framework-aware Levels, one context per learner/Language (at most two ACTIVE), ACTIVE/PAUSED lifecycle, context-owned progress/preferences, account-global Profile/streak and learner+Vocabulary SRS. Derived LearningPlan is deterministic, not persisted by default; no Enrollment, persisted SkillProfile or mastery engine is introduced. ADR-032 retains three entry paths and completed-result-only recommendation. ADR-033 retains mostly sequential core lessons, skipped-starting-point ≠ completed, course-configured readiness and local-day qualifying activity. ADR-034/035 retain exactly three single roles, assigned Teacher authoring, Admin governance, protected subject/status, minimum audit and trusted grading/scheduling. Earlier ADR “open” task statuses and classified historical requirement rows do not override later scoped approvals.
