APPROVED

## Human-Approved Resolution

- A trusted application/runtime service creates signed URLs; clients cannot create signed URLs themselves.
- Signed URL TTL is exactly one hour for vocabulary audio and Profile avatars.
- Storage uses one private bucket named `lingora-media`; the bucket is not public and anonymous access is denied.
- Vocabulary audio path: `vocabulary/{vocabularyId}/{filename}`.
- Profile avatar path: `profiles/{userId}/{filename}`.
- Vocabulary audio permits `audio/mpeg` and `audio/wav`, with a maximum size of 10 MB.
- Profile avatars permit `image/jpeg`, `image/png`, and `image/webp`, with a maximum size of 5 MB.
- Profile avatar Storage is in M1.3. Learners may upload, replace, and delete only their own avatar objects.
- Vocabulary audio is ADMIN/service-managed. Learners receive read-only delivery through the trusted signed-URL flow.
- Existing objects are never overwritten. Replacement uses a new object with a new filename/version.
- Learners may delete only their own avatar objects. Vocabulary audio may be deleted only by ADMIN/service actors.
- A vocabulary-audio signed URL is issued only when the vocabulary is reachable through published `Course → Lesson → LessonVocabulary` content.
