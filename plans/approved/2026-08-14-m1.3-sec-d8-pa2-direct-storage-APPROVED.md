# M1.3 SEC-D8 PA2 — Direct Authenticated Storage + RLS-Only

## Status

APPROVED

This human-approved amendment supersedes only the signed-URL runtime and TTL portions of the earlier SEC-D8 addendum. Historical snapshots remain unchanged.

## Approved Amendment

- Authenticated clients access permitted objects in the private `lingora-media` bucket directly under RLS.
- M1.3 does not implement a trusted signed-URL runtime.
- M1.3 does not enforce or test a one-hour signed-URL TTL.
- The Storage capability implied by permissions granted to an authenticated client is accepted under PA2.
- Anonymous access and public-bucket delivery remain denied.
- Vocabulary audio remains at `vocabulary/{vocabularyId}/{filename}`, with `audio/mpeg` or `audio/wav` and a 10 MB maximum.
- Profile avatars remain at `profiles/{userId}/{filename}`, with `image/jpeg`, `image/png`, or `image/webp` and a 5 MB maximum.
- Learners may upload, read, replace, and delete only their own avatar objects.
- Vocabulary audio is ADMIN/service-managed. Authenticated learners have read-only access only when the vocabulary is reachable through published `Course → Lesson → LessonVocabulary` content.
- Existing objects are never overwritten; replacement uses a new filename/version.
- Learners may delete only their own avatars; only ADMIN/service actors may delete vocabulary audio.

## Scope Boundary

- Authorization is implemented through bucket configuration, grants and RLS policies only.
- No API, Edge Function, signed-URL service, Android/UI, ML/NLP component or dependency is introduced by this amendment.
- Any new semantic gap returns M1.3 to REVIEW.
