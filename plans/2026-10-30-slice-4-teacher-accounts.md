## Verified domain / owner decisions

- app.Account: UUID PK supplied from auth.users.id; SQL FK RESTRICT (not Prisma-modeled Auth relation), fixed role string ADMIN/TEACHER/LEARNER, status PENDING(default)/ACTIVE/DISABLED; nullable displayName/nativeLanguageId/avatarMediaId/activatedAt; uiLocale=en FK to existing Language.code, timezone=UTC nonblank; createdAt/updatedAt, positive BigInt rowVersion(default1). No email column/natural unique key; email belongs to Auth. Account has numerous incoming RESTRICT content/assignment/audit-actor FKs; no product delete lifecycle. Baseline SQL guard_account_role_fixed prevents role updates; audit_events is append-only. Do not change these guards.
- Existing verifyStaffIdentity performs fresh provider getUser + fresh Account read, requires ACTIVE staff, then required ADMIN/TEACHER. App login verifies status after password sign-in and signs out inactive accounts; /holding requires active Teacher (Admin redirects). Disable is app status ACTIVE->DISABLED, next protected request403 ACCOUNT_INACTIVE even with a valid existing session; login to app is denied. Do not ban Auth user or revoke sessions: Auth identity/session may remain valid, but app access is denied until re-enable; DISABLED->ACTIVE restores app eligibility with normal valid Auth session. Preserve activatedAt on re-enable. PENDING is invitation/first-use activation, not an Admin enable shortcut.
- app_private.ProvisioningRequest already exists: unique operationKey UUID and normalizedEmail, requestedRole TEACHER/ADMIN, requestedBy Account FK, authUserId FK auth.users RESTRICT, state REQUESTED/IDENTITY_CREATED/READY/FAILED_RETRYABLE, providerCorrelationId text, lastErrorCode, timestamps/rowVersion. Use these actual fields for narrow provisioning recovery; no new tables/queue/framework. AuditEvent already exists and append-only: use minimal lifecycle events, no audit UI/secrets/email/password in safeContext.
- Owner agreed to configure a server-only SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY or SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY (not yet assumed present), and explicitly authorized a minimal first-use Teacher invitation/password activation flow. Existing app supports password login only; no invitation callback/activation route. Owner mailbox/SMTP and redirect configuration remain live prerequisites; local implementation/tests can proceed.
